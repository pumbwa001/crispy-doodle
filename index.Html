Welcome to Light Speed

Light Speed is your best choice for a simple, convenient way to capture pictures, turn text in images into copyable words, keep track of pictures, and practice recognizing handwriting—all in one place.

Use the camera for a quick capture, Saved pictures to manage or import images, and Teach handwriting to try a simple character-recognition exercise. You can copy or share converted text with other apps, or download it as a text file when system sharing is unavailable.

Camera and text recognition

  1. Choose the camera button and allow camera access when your browser asks. Light Speed only uses the camera while the camera screen is open.
  2. Place the writing in good light, hold the camera steady, and choose Capture & save. Choose Retake if you want another picture.
  3. Light Speed saves the picture in Saved pictures and tries to recognize text in it. Use the case buttons to change the displayed text, Maximize text to enlarge it, Copy text to copy it, or Share text to send it to another app.

Camera access generally requires a secure website connection (HTTPS) or localhost. If the camera does not start, check your browser's site permissions and make sure another app is not using it. Text recognition happens in this browser; results can be imperfect, especially for handwriting, blurry images, or low light.

Saved pictures

  • Captured pictures are kept in this browser's local Saved pictures library. On browsers that support folder access, you can also choose a folder to save or view files there.
  • On browsers without folder access, captures are downloaded to your device. You can use Add image to import pictures into the local library.
  • Choose Convert to soft copy to recognize text in a saved image. Samples saved in Teach handwriting can help with clear, separated letters, numbers, and punctuation marks. Expand the text box, change letter case, copy the result, or share it with another app.
  • Choose Delete picture to permanently remove an image. Confirm the prompt before it is deleted. Deleting a library picture removes it from this app; deleting a folder picture removes the file from the selected folder.

Saved-library pictures stay in this browser on this device; they are not automatically synced or backed up. Clearing browser site data can remove them. Downloaded or folder-saved copies are separate files.

Teach handwriting

  1. Select a letter, number, or punctuation mark and draw it in the box with your finger, mouse, or stylus.
  2. Choose Save sample to add that drawing to the on-screen training library. Save more examples of a character to give the recognizer more examples to compare.
  3. Draw a character and choose Test recognition to compare it with the saved samples. Use Clear to clear the drawing or Reset training to clear all saved samples.

This is an experimental pattern-matching aid, not a replacement for the text reader or a full handwriting model. Saved samples stay in this browser on this device and may help with clear, separated characters during picture conversion. It only adjusts characters when the image can be split into characters and a sample match is clear; connected cursive, unusual fonts, or unclear images may not match. Recognition can still be wrong, so check the result. The original text reader is used whenever a sample match is uncertain. Reset training removes your saved samples.

Privacy and using Light Speed

Pictures, converted text, and handwriting samples are handled on your device by the app; camera permission is controlled by your browser. Sharing opens your device's share menu when supported. Otherwise, Light Speed downloads converted text as a .txt file that you can open or attach in another app. Keep a separate copy of important pictures, and only grant camera or folder access when you choose to use those features. For the best experience on a phone, open Light Speed in an up-to-date browser and allow camera access when prompted.
